IOStream are different from StreamApi

whereas StreamApi is used to process the collections of objects.

PipeLine:
-----------------------------------------------
A PipeLine in StreamAPI is a sequence of Stream Operations

This Operations like filter,map,collect etc are connected
and executed as chain of operations

PipeLine:
Collection->Stream->operation->Result

These Operations process the data from a source a produces a final result.
StreamAPI supports to types of operations:
1)Intermediate Operations

Intermediate Operations allows us to transform or filter the objects before the final result
is produced
These Operations return another stream object as the result
these operations can be chained one after the other.

2)Terminal Operations

Terminal Operations will Start/trigger the execution of the entire stream pipeline.

they produce a result such as a collection,they do not return a Stream/
examples of terminal operations:
collect,forEach,count,reduce,min,max,anyMatch,allMatch
Terminal Operations tell that the execution of entire stream in pipeline 


lazy evaluation vs Eager evaluation:

Lazy evauation is used by intermediate operations like
-map,filter,sorted etc

these operations are called lazy because they are not executed immediatley when the stream is created
they are just recored to run later

Actual operations starts only when terminal operation like collect,forEach,count etc is invoked

they are called lazy beacuase they just build a pipeline and delays the execution until needed.


Eager Evaluation:
-Terminal operations like collect,forEach will trigger the execution of all the intermediate operations
-as soon a terminal operations is called,it immediately executes the intermediate functions/operations

that why terminal operations are called eager operations.
